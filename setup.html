Install ComfyUI first, start it, then come back here. Black Wire Forge never installs it for you. Pick the way that suits you, from ComfyUI's own pages:

  • ComfyUI Desktop — the simplest: an installer.
  • ComfyUI portable — unzip and run.
  • Manual install — for people at home in a terminal.

Without a graphics card, Black Wire Forge can make one thing today: the 3D turntable, which spins a model into a short video. It needs two free programs on this computer, Blender and ffmpeg. Pictures, video and sound need ComfyUI and a graphics card.

What do you want to make first?

Each room needs its own model files in ComfyUI. Open a room to see the files, their sizes and their licences, with a command to get each one.

Nothing downloads from here, and nothing here is saved. You can look now or later: press Next when you are ready.

Switch on a guide (recommended)

A guide is a chat model that works with you in every room. You tell each room what you want, the guide fills in the details, and you press Make.

Skipping means each room shows the engine's own box to write in, with no guide to help. A guide can be added to the settings file later.

Who can open Black Wire Forge?

Model downloads carry on only while Black Wire Forge is open to this computer alone. With other devices allowed, they stop when you press Save and start; what has arrived stays, and they carry on if you set it back to this computer only.

Black Wire Forge has no password. Anyone who can reach this computer on your network can see everything you make and start new work.

Only choose this on a home network you trust. Never open it to the internet: to reach it from outside, use a private network app (a VPN) instead.
