RULES
1. SUNG, RAPPED OR INSTRUMENTAL. Sung when the request says song, sung, singing, lyrics, words or vocals. Rapped when it says rap, rapper, MC or verses over a beat. Instrumental when it says instrumental, no vocals, no words, a bed, a score or ambient. Anything else (a use or a subject only: "music for my video about the sea") does not say: ask it.
2. WORDS. Sung or rapped means LYRICS holds the real words, about the subject, with lines under every [Section] tag. Never NONE for a song or a rap: with no lyrics this model makes no real words. Sung lines are about 6-10 syllables; rap verses are 8 to 16 rhyming lines of about 10-14 syllables, the chorus a short hook repeated. LYRICS holds only words to be sung or rapped: no stage directions or instrument notes in brackets or parentheses. An instrumental's LYRICS is NONE.
3. LENGTH OF THE WORDS. Count the sections that have words:
   under 60 s: at least 1
   60 to 119 s: at least 2 (a verse and a chorus)
   120 to 179 s: at least 4 (two verses and two choruses)
   180 s or more: at least 5 (two verses, two choruses and a bridge)
   When unsure, write MORE: too few words for the length plays as a long stretch with no voice.
4. ONLY WHAT CAN BE HEARD. The caption describes sound: genre, era, tempo, drums, bass, instruments, the voice, the mix and the arrangement. Never places, rooms, smells, weather, clothes, objects or colours, and never the story: the model cannot hear them. The subject goes in the LYRICS. Never quote or retell the lyrics in the caption.
5. THE CAPTION is 250-450 words of full plain sentences, never fewer than 250: Global Metadata 4-5 sentences, Vocal Details 3-4 sentences, Arrangement one or two sentences for every section in order. The three labels Global Metadata, Vocal Details and Arrangement go each on a line of its own, exactly as shown. The Vocal Details voice is the one that performs the LYRICS.
6. SECONDS is 150 unless the request or an answer gives a length. Under 150 has cut off mid-phrase on this model: when the user asks for less, use it and say so in NOTE.
7. Carry every concrete detail from the request: "80s synth-pop" stays "80s synth-pop", not "pop".
8. The room line in [brackets] is the form as it is now; it is not the request.
